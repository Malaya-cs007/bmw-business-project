Our Story

One Plant. Three Perspectives. A Shared Goal.

BMW Manufacturing's Spartanburg facility brings together people, technology, and production processes to build vehicles for customers in the United States and around the world. Its work connects manufacturing quality with efficient operations and coordinated business decisions.

Our group chose this business to explore a question: what does it take to keep a vehicle manufacturing plant running smoothly? We approached it through three connected careers: IT support, operations management, and financial analysis.

As a student team, our goal is to show how reliable technology, organized production, and informed financial planning work together to support a successful business.

This is an educational website created by students. The team below represents our assigned project roles, not BMW employees or founders.

Meet The Project Team

Malaya Coley-Sturgis

Malaya's project role focuses on troubleshooting technical problems, supporting employees, and keeping devices and systems working reliably. Her role shows how dependable technology supports communication and daily production.

Abby Oehlmann

Operations Manager

Abby's project role focuses on coordinating people, production schedules, and resources. Her role connects departments, identifies opportunities to improve efficiency, and keeps production goals and quality standards in focus.

Jacob Seigler

Financial Analyst

Jacob's project role focuses on tracking budgets, reviewing operating costs, and analyzing financial performance. His role shows how financial information helps management plan resources and make informed decisions.

How Our Roles Work Together

If a production system experiences a technical problem, IT support helps restore access. Operations management coordinates the production response and adjusts schedules as needed. Financial analysis examines how the interruption affects costs and budgets. Together, these roles help the business solve problems and meet its goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy a vehicle through this website?

This website is a class project and does not accept orders. You can explore our group's vehicle offerings on the Products & Services page.

Why did our group choose manufacturing?

Manufacturing connects technology, operations, and finance. It lets each group member explore a different career while showing how those roles depend on one another.

Who should I contact about a technical problem?

In our project scenario, Malaya handles IT support. Abby considers the effect on production, and Jacob evaluates any related costs.

Does the team build cars during class?

Only in our business plans! Our actual tools are research, teamwork, HTML, and CSS.
